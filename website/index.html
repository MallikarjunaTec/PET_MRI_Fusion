Fusion Method

Select a fusion algorithm to combine MRI and PET modalities.

Weighted Fusion
Combines MRI and PET using a simple weighted average. Equal weights preserve balanced structural and metabolic information.
PCA Fusion
Uses Principal Component Analysis to determine optimal fusion weights from the data covariance structure.
Wavelet Fusion
Decomposes images using the Haar wavelet transform. Low-frequency components are averaged while high-frequency details are selected by maximum absolute value.
Improved Wavelet Fusion
Enhanced wavelet fusion using a 2-level Daubechies-2 decomposition. Provides better frequency separation and preserves finer structural details.

About This Project

Understanding PET–MRI brain image fusion and its applications.

🧲 MRI — Structural Imaging

Magnetic Resonance Imaging provides high-resolution anatomical detail of brain structures. It excels at differentiating soft tissues, showing gray matter, white matter, and cerebrospinal fluid with excellent spatial resolution.

☢️ PET — Functional Imaging

Positron Emission Tomography reveals metabolic and functional activity in the brain. It measures glucose metabolism, blood flow, and neurotransmitter activity, providing crucial information that structural imaging cannot capture.

🔬 Fusion — Combined Insight

Image fusion combines complementary information from both modalities into a single image. The fused image provides both structural detail and functional context, enabling more comprehensive brain analysis.

Fusion Methods

Weighted Fusion

Simple weighted average combining MRI structure and PET metabolic data with equal or user-defined weights.

PCA Fusion

Data-driven weighting using principal component analysis to capture the most important information from both modalities.

Wavelet Fusion

Haar wavelet decomposition with averaged low-frequency and max-selected high-frequency components.

Improved Wavelet

Enhanced 2-level Daubechies-2 wavelet decomposition for finer frequency separation and better detail preservation.

⚕️ Research & Educational Use Only

This application is intended for research and educational purposes and is not a diagnostic medical device. It must not be used for clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions.

📖 How to Use

1. Upload Brain Scans

  • Upload an MRI NIfTI file (.nii or .nii.gz)
  • Upload a PET NIfTI file (.nii or .nii.gz)

2. Preview Images

  • Navigate through axial, coronal, and sagittal views
  • Use the slice slider to browse through the volume

3. Select Fusion Method

  • Choose from Weighted, PCA, Wavelet, or Improved Wavelet fusion
  • Or compare all methods at once

4. View Results

  • Examine the fused brain image
  • Compare MRI, PET, and Fused side by side
  • Review quantitative metrics

5. Export

  • Download fused NIfTI, metrics reports, and visualizations

Supported Formats

  • NIfTI-1 (.nii and .nii.gz)
  • 3D brain volumes only
